Greenfield University Student Conduct & Campus Life Policy (Fictional — Demonstration Document)

This policy applies to all enrolled students and recognized student organizations at Greenfield University. Greenfield University is fictional and exists solely for product demonstration.

1. Community standards
Students are expected to uphold honesty, respect, and safety in academic and residential settings. Conduct rules apply on campus, in university-sponsored online spaces, and at official university events off campus.

2. Reporting safety incidents
Students must report safety incidents within twenty-four (24) hours to campus safety staff. Reports may be submitted through the safety hotline, mobile app, or in person at the campus safety office. Anonymous tips are accepted when permitted by law, though follow-up may be limited.

3. Residence life
Residence hall quiet hours run from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM during instructional periods. Guest policies require hosts to register overnight visitors. Damage to university property may result in restitution and conduct charges.

4. Academic integrity
Plagiarism, cheating, fabrication, and unauthorized collaboration are prohibited. Sanctions range from educational interventions to suspension or expulsion depending on severity and prior findings.

5. Anti-harassment and nondiscrimination
Anti-harassment standards apply to campus and online spaces under student conduct rules. Discrimination based on protected characteristics is prohibited. Supportive measures may be offered during investigations.

6. Alcohol and drugs
Possession or use of illegal drugs is prohibited. Alcohol is restricted to designated areas and events approved by the university. Intoxication that endangers others may result in interim measures.

7. Weapons and hazardous materials
Weapons are prohibited except where expressly allowed by law and university authorization. Unauthorized experiments with hazardous materials in residence halls are prohibited.

8. Freedom of expression
Peaceful protest is permitted subject to time, place, and manner rules that protect safety and university operations. Amplified sound may require permits.

9. Digital conduct
Harassment, doxxing, and targeted intimidation using university networks or directed at university community members may violate this policy even when originating off campus if it causes substantial disruption.

10. Organizations and hazing
Registered student organizations must follow advisor requirements and financial controls. Hazing in any form is prohibited and may lead to organization suspension and individual sanctions.

11. Appeals and timelines
Appeals must be filed within seven (7) calendar days after written notice of a conduct decision. Appeals are limited to procedural fairness, sufficiency of evidence, and proportionality of sanctions except where law requires broader review.

12. Accommodations
The campus schedules accommodation review meetings within ten (10) business days of receiving complete documentation. Students awaiting decisions should coordinate with faculty for interim academic arrangements as appropriate.

13. Interim measures
The university may impose no-contact orders, housing reassignment, or interim suspensions when necessary to protect community safety pending investigation outcomes.

14. Sanctions
Sanctions include warnings, probation, restitution, educational assignments, housing removal, suspension, and expulsion. Repeated major misconduct can lead to suspension or expulsion.

15. Compliance with sanctions
Failure to comply with sanctions may escalate penalties under the conduct process. Noncompliance includes missing assigned meetings, refusing restitution plans without approved dispute, or violating no-contact orders.

16. Facilities access
Unauthorized access to labs or residence facilities is prohibited. Tailgating through secured entrances, sharing access cards, or propping secure doors open may result in disciplinary action and loss of facility access.

17. Retaliation
Retaliation against reporting parties is prohibited and may trigger immediate interim measures. Retaliation includes threats, social ostracism campaigns coordinated to harm, and adverse academic coordination outside legitimate grading.

18. Student employment
Student employees must follow both student conduct rules and employment policies. Conflicts are resolved through joint review by Student Conduct and Human Resources.

19. Research integrity
Research misconduct allegations are referred to the research integrity officer under separate procedures that may run parallel to student conduct processes when applicable.

20. Supplemental campus operations (fictional)
Campus safety publishes annual security reports summarizing incidents and prevention programs.

Emergency notifications may be sent via text, email, and public address systems.

Lost and found is managed through the student union desk.

Parking violations are handled administratively and are not typically student conduct matters unless tied to safety risks.

Animals in campus housing require disability-related approval or documented compliance with pet policy exceptions.

Outdoor events require facilities scheduling and risk review.

Student media must follow advertising disclosure rules.

International students must maintain visa-related enrollment requirements coordinated with the international office.

Fraternity and sorority chapters must maintain national organization compliance and university recognition standards.

Campus IT may block devices that repeatedly violate acceptable use policies.

Library conduct rules prohibit harassment, theft, and misuse of collections.

Athletics participants must follow NCAA-style fictional league rules described in the athletics handbook supplement.

Performing arts venues have additional safety rules for rigging and props.

Laboratory courses require completion of safety modules before access.

Field trips require signed waivers and attendance policies communicated in advance.

Student government elections follow the election commission calendar.

Volunteer service hours may be recognized on co-curricular transcripts when documented.

Campus dining plans are governed by separate contracts with refund windows.

Summer housing has modified guest and quiet hour rules posted each year.

Commuter students are expected to follow the same conduct standards while participating in university programs.

This fictional policy is labeled for demonstration use only.
