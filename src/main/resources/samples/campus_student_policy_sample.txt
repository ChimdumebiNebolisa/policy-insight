Greenfield Campus Student Policy (Fictional)

Students must report safety incidents within twenty-four (24) hours to campus safety staff.
Residence hall quiet hours run from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM during instructional periods.
Appeals must be filed within seven (7) calendar days after written notice.

The campus schedules accommodation review meetings within ten (10) business days of receiving complete documentation.
Anti-harassment standards apply to campus and online spaces under student conduct rules.

Unauthorized access to labs or residence facilities is prohibited.
Retaliation against reporting parties is prohibited and may trigger immediate interim measures.

Repeated major misconduct can lead to suspension or expulsion.
Failure to comply with sanctions may escalate penalties under the conduct process.
