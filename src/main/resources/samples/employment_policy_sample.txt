Northbridge Employment Policy (Fictional)

Employees must complete annual conduct training by March 31 each calendar year.
Timesheets must be submitted by Monday 10:00 AM local time, and payroll adjustments depend on approved timesheets.

Managers must acknowledge leave requests within five (5) business days and document approvals in the HR portal.
Employees must disclose conflicts of interest in writing within five (5) business days of identifying a conflict.

Employees are prohibited from storing confidential customer data on personal devices.
Remote work from unapproved countries is not allowed without written compliance authorization.

Unauthorized overtime may result in corrective action through progressive discipline up to termination.
Policy violations involving harassment may result in immediate termination.
