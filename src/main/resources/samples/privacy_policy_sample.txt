BrightLeaf Services Privacy Policy (Fictional)

We collect account profile details, device diagnostics, and service interaction logs to provide support and improve platform reliability.
We collect account profile details only for account operations, fraud prevention, and product quality.
We do not sell personal data to third-party advertisers.

We retain personal data for twenty-four (24) months from the last account activity unless legal hold requests may suspend deletion.
Users may request deletion through privacy@brightleaf.example after identity verification, and we respond to verified requests within forty-five (45) days.

Security teams monitor security logs for unauthorized access and investigate incidents under documented procedures.
Access to raw support transcripts is restricted to trained support leads.

If we materially change this policy, we notify users at least thirty (30) days before new terms take effect.
